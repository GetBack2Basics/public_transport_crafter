How to Use NSW Transit Crafter (ELI10 Guide)

Welcome! Have you ever taken a bus to a train station, only to watch your train pull away right as you stepped off? That stinks! NSW Transit Crafter is a smart map and timetable tool that fixes that problem without spending any extra taxpayer money.

🧒 ELI10 (Explain Like I'm 10): Imagine you and your friend want to trade Pokémon cards at the school gate at 3:15 PM. If your bus always gets there at 3:17 PM, you keep missing each other! But what if the bus driver left just 3 minutes earlier? Then you'd both meet on time without needing a second bus. That is exactly what this tool does for thousands of buses and trains all across New South Wales!

The map shows the exact street turns the bus drives on (no straight zigzag lines across buildings!). The green dotted circle shows a 1-kilometer walking zone around the stop, highlighting train stations, metro stops, and ferry wharves you can easily walk to.

The system looks at every single trip from morning until night. It gives each bus trip a small nudge (like leaving 5 minutes later or 3 minutes earlier) so passengers have 7 to 17 minutes to walk to the train platform. Nobody has to run for their life, and nobody is stuck freezing on the platform for 45 minutes!

4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Why doesn't this require any extra buses or drivers?

A: The math solver uses a rule called Zero Added Fleet. If an operator has 2 buses driving a route, the solver is only allowed to shift when those same 2 buses depart by a few minutes. It never asks for a 3rd bus!

Q: Can I check any bus in the state, even out in regional NSW?

A: Yes! All 10,266 public transit routes across Sydney, the Blue Mountains, Central Coast, Hunter, Illawarra, and Regional NSW are included.

Q: Why does the website load fast with zero server costs?

A: The website runs completely in your web browser using DuckDB SQL and static Google Cloud Storage hosting. When nobody is using it, it costs $0.00 to keep on standby.

5. Behind the Scenes: Architecture & Zero-Mock Defense

Now that you know how to use the system, here is how the magic happens under the hood!

🧒 ELI10 (Zero-Mock Defense): In computer programming, people sometimes cheat by making up "fake data" (like rolling a pair of dice to generate random fake bus arrival times). We strictly forbid this. Our automated robot scanners inspect every line of code to prove 100% of the numbers come from Transport for NSW government open data feeds!
Figure 9: Zero-Mock Data Integrity Certificate 🔍 Click to enlarge

How Data Flows from NSW Open Data to Your Screen:

  1. NSW Open Data Feeds: The system downloads official GTFS timetables, real Opal card tap-on/tap-off transaction counts, and road traffic congestion statistics.
  2. Lakehouse Parquet Files: Data is saved into 45 high-speed Apache Parquet tables on Google Cloud Storage. Each file is verified with special PAR1 bytes.
  3. In-Browser DuckDB SQL: When you search a route, your browser runs real SQL queries directly against the cloud files.
  4. Road Snapping: Bus stops are joined along physical road lines so turns and intersections match reality.
  5. Constraint Solver: Calculates the exact minute adjustment ($\Delta t$) to make connections click together.
