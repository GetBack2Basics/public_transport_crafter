How to Use NSW Transit Crafter (User Guide)

Welcome! Have you ever taken a bus to a train station, only to watch the train pull away right as you stepped off? That's the worst! NSW Transit Crafter is a smart map and timetable optimization platform that fixes missed connections across New South Wales without needing any extra taxpayer dollars.

💡 The Everyday Problem: Imagine you live in a suburb with just one bus route. Every morning, your bus arrives at the train station 5 minutes after the all-stops train has already departed. That means you're stuck waiting a whole hour on the platform for the next train!

Now imagine: what if the bus simply left your neighborhood 8 minutes earlier? You would arrive with plenty of time to walk to the platform, hop straight onto the train, and save nearly an hour every single day. The bus company doesn't need to buy a second bus or hire an extra driver—we just shift the departure time slightly so the bus and train actually talk to each other!

4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Why doesn't this require any extra buses or drivers?

A: The math solver uses a rule called Zero Added Fleet. If an operator has 2 buses driving a route, the solver is only allowed to shift when those same 2 buses depart by a few minutes. It never asks for a 3rd bus!

Q: Can I check any bus in the state, even out in regional NSW?

A: Yes! All 10,266 public transit routes across Sydney, the Blue Mountains, Central Coast, Hunter, Illawarra, and Regional NSW are included.

Q: Why does the website load fast with zero server costs?

A: The website runs completely in your web browser using DuckDB SQL and static Google Cloud Storage hosting. When nobody is using it, it costs $0.00 to keep on standby.

5. Behind the Scenes: Architecture & Zero-Mock Defense

Now that you know how to use the system, here is how the magic happens under the hood!

🛡️ Zero Fake Data Guarantee: In computer modeling, teams sometimes make up numbers (like rolling dice to generate simulated arrival times). We strictly forbid this. Our automated scanners inspect every line of code to prove 100% of the numbers come from Transport for NSW government open data feeds!
Figure 9: Zero-Mock Data Integrity Certificate 🔍 Click to enlarge

How Data Flows from NSW Open Data to Your Screen:

  1. NSW Open Data Feeds: The system downloads official GTFS timetables, real Opal card tap-on/tap-off transaction counts, and road traffic congestion statistics.
  2. Lakehouse Parquet Files: Data is saved into 45 high-speed Apache Parquet tables on Google Cloud Storage. Each file is verified with special PAR1 bytes.
  3. In-Browser DuckDB SQL: When you search a route, your browser runs real SQL queries directly against the cloud files.
  4. Road Snapping: Bus stops are joined along physical road lines so turns and intersections match reality.
  5. Constraint Solver: Calculates the exact minute adjustment ($\Delta t$) to make connections click together.

Official Transport for NSW Symbology & Brand Standards

The platform strictly implements the design guidelines and assets provided by Transport for NSW:

  • Transport Mode Symbols and Pictograms Dataset : Mode identifiers follow the standard letter roundels ([T] Train, [M] Metro, [B] Bus, [F] Ferry, [L] Light Rail, [C] Coach) for all spatial map pins, legend indicators, and tabular badges.
  • Transport Network Colour Chart (TNCC) : Mode colors are mapped directly from official developer resources: Train (#F99D1C Melon Yellow / PMS 137C), Metro (#168388 Teal / PMS 321), Bus (#00B5E2 Transport Blue), Ferry (#00774B Manly Green / PMS 3415C), Light Rail (#E4002B Red), and Regional Coach (#732A82 Purple / PMS 7657C).
